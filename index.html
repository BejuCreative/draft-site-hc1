The Be The Cause® Mind Map

Your life story follows a map. Nine stations. One journey from wound to unity. Once you can see it, you can stop reacting to the past and start causing a better future.

“I made that. I created that.” — Dr. Judy, on the Mind Map she painted

  1. Encoding
  2. Decoding
  3. Recoding

Encoding

01

The Wound

The wounds of childhood cast shadows over our mental well-being, putting us in a reactive state.

Encoding

02

Reaction

When we are wounded, our lens of perception cracks, our reality distorts, and in reaction we either fight, flee, freeze or fawn.

Encoding

03

Encoding

We encode negative core beliefs that act as emotional triggers. Beliefs we buy into, like “I’m not good enough” or “I’m not lovable.”

Decoding

04

Chaos

When negative core beliefs are triggered, they break us down into chaos.

Decoding

05

Breakdown / Breakthrough

When we can no longer defend ourselves through dysfunctional means, we break down — which is an opportunity to break through.

Decoding

06

Defenses

We erect defenses to protect ourselves against pain. However, some defenses lock us into psychological prison.

Recoding

07

Paradigm Shift

You break through, heal your cracked lens of perception, and arrive at a new way of seeing and a new way of being. “The reclamation of your true authentic self.”

Recoding

08

Healing

As you recode into health, you evolve into a better version of yourself.

Recoding

09

Unity

When you break the multigenerational encoding, you can Be The Cause® of better outcomes for your life — and pay it forward to the next generation.

About

Dr. Judy Rosenberg

Licensed Clinical Psychologist · Founder of the Psychological Healing Center · Creator of the Be the Cause® Mind Map System

Dr. Judy Rosenberg is a licensed clinical psychologist and the founder of the Psychological Healing Center. With more than 30 years of experience, she has dedicated her career to helping people heal from narcissistic abuse and childhood wounds.

Her specialty is getting to the root cause of symptoms rather than simply managing them. Dr. Judy helps people understand that those root causes are often part of a multigenerational blueprint passed down through families — and that lasting healing comes from recognizing and changing that blueprint.

She is the creator of the Be the Cause® Mind Map System and the author of Be the Cause: Healing Human Disconnect. She completed her undergraduate work in psychology at UCLA and her graduate work at CGI (California Graduate Institute), and has been in private practice since 1996.
